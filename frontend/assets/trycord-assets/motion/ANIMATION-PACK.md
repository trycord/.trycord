# Trycord Ember animation pack

## Direction: Ember Signal

Motion is structural and communicative: an Ember seam travels through dark architectural space, then comes to rest. The merged Trycord mark remains one indivisible form in every animation—never separated into Greek letters or independent pieces.

## Included GIFs

- `brand/trycord-symbol-reveal.gif` — whole-mark reveal with a short Ember seam pass.
- `app/trycord-app-icon-pulse.gif` — restrained app-icon emphasis loop.
- `motion/trycord-loader.gif` — raster companion to `loader.svg`.
- `motion/trycord-typing-indicator.gif` — raster companion to `typing-indicator.css`.
- `motion/trycord-skeleton-shimmer.gif` — raster companion to `skeleton.css`.
- `motion/trycord-community-signal.gif` — community/federation signal motif based on `community-signal.svg`.

## Use

Use GIFs where a raster animation is required (social, fallback contexts, chat embeds). Prefer the original SVG/CSS files in product UI for sharp rendering, theme control, accessibility, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. All loops are restrained and contain no bounce, glow, letter separation, or invented logo geometry.
