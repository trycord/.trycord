Trust & Safety

How Trycord handles abuse, reports, and the people who get hurt by it. Each instance sets its own rules; this is the baseline the software is built to support.

Reporting

Report from inside the app. A report reaches the moderators of the community it happened in, and on an official instance it also escalates to the project. Reports are not anonymous to moderators: they include who filed them, because that is usually needed to act.

What the software enforces

  • Permission checks on every request, not only in the interface.
  • A role hierarchy, so a moderator cannot act on someone who outranks them.
  • File type and size limits on uploads.
  • Rate limits on registration and messaging.

Enforcement decisions are made by people, not only by software. That is deliberate, and it is also why every instance must offer an appeal route — see Support.

Appeals

Anyone actioned can appeal at the appeal form, signed in or not. On official instances appeals are reviewed centrally, so a community you were removed from cannot refuse to hear you.

What we ask of you

Report in good faith, and say what you actually saw. Do not report someone to settle a disagreement, and do not coordinate reports to pressure an instance. Both make a moderation system worse for everyone living inside it.
