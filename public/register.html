Join this instance

A place that answers to its people.

Your account belongs to this instance, and this instance answers for it. One identity follows you into every community you join here.

  • 01 One account, every community. Join the servers that are open to you and keep the same name and people.
  • 02 Your address stays private. An email is for recovery and verification. It is never shown on your profile.
  • 03 You can leave. Export your account, or close it, without asking somebody else's platform for permission.
