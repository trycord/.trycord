What the operator fills in

Anything Trycord cannot know is an operator field. Each one needs its own stable data-op name:

  • Contact address: OPERATOR: the address people should contact you on
  • Legal entity, if you have one: OPERATOR: your registered name, or omit this line
  • Jurisdiction: OPERATOR: the country or state your instance operates under

The all-caps prefix is the placeholder. It tells whoever is setting this up that the value is still missing, and it disappears as soon as the field is filled in.
