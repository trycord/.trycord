Confirming an address proves the mailbox is yours, so recovery can reach you and the instance can trust where it sends account mail.

  • 01 It is not a sign-in. Confirming an address does not ask for your password and does not open a session by itself.
  • 02 The link works once. If it has expired, sign in and ask for another from your account settings.
  • 03 Only the address changes. Your username, communities, and messages stay exactly where they are.
