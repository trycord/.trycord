Contact

Trycord has no central operator — each instance is run by the people who host it. Here is how to reach the right people for the right question.

For the project itself

Bugs, feature requests, and general questions about the software belong on the GitHub repository: github.com/LanxTheShowmaker/.trycord/issues.

For people on this instance

If you have an account on this server and something goes wrong — a moderation decision, a data question, a report — the maintainers of this instance are the first place to ask. Report problems from inside the app or raise them with your instance's administrator.

Legal or privacy questions

See the placeholder notices on the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages. On this instance those documents have not been finalized, so for anything legally significant, contact this instance's operator directly rather than relying on the drafts.

Operator placeholder. The person running this instance can edit public/contact.html to add their own contact details — for example an address or an operator email — before the site goes live.
