The product

Features

Trycord keeps the surface small and does the important things well. Everything below ships with the web client and the desktop app.

Messaging

  • Communities — your own spaces, each with its own channels, members, and permissions.
  • Text channels — organized, persistent conversation in each community.
  • Direct messages — one-to-one conversations outside any community.
  • Friends & presence — keep the people you talk to most nearby and see when they are online.

Content

  • Rich messages — send, edit, and delete messages, with edit history and permission controls around who may change or remove what.
  • Attachments — images and files up to 8 MB per message.
  • Safe layout — uploaded images render sandboxed into the page, not as raw HTML.

Profiles

  • Display name, status, and bio shown across the app.
  • Avatar and banner — your own picture and page background.
  • Nicknames per community — members can address you the way you prefer in each space.
  • Verified recovery settings — add a recovery email, verify it by a code the server sends you, and use it to reset your password.

Administration

  • Permissions — granular control over who can do what inside a community.
  • Reports & appeals — members flag problems; moderators and admins act on them.
  • Platform admins — designated users can manage accounts and enforce policy across the whole instance.
  • Suspensions — suspend accounts for violations, with an appeal path.

Security & operations

  • JWT sessions with server-side session management.
  • Rate limiting on auth and messaging endpoints.
  • A strict Content-Security-Policy on every page: no inline scripts, no cross-origin framing.
  • Uniform JSON error envelopes so clients always know what failed and why.
  • SQLite for simple setups, MySQL for production, both configured solely from environment variables.
  • Registration requires explicit acceptance of the current Terms of Service Official instance terms and Privacy Policy versions.
